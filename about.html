About Us

Who we are and what we do.

Who We Are

Circuit Dr. A is an advanced chip-level PCB repair laboratory. We specialize in repairing — not replacing — electronic control boards used in home appliances, electric vehicles, solar systems, and industrial equipment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make quality PCB repair accessible and affordable. Instead of forcing customers to buy expensive new boards, we restore the original board at chip level, saving both money and the environment.

What We Repair

  • Inverter AC & Refrigerator PCB
  • Washing Machine & Home Appliance PCB
  • Lithium Battery BMS & Energy Storage
  • EV & Automotive Electronics (EV & Auto ECU)
  • Solar Inverter, UPS & Power Backup
  • LED / Smart TV & Display Motherboard
  • Industrial & Commercial Electronics PCB

Our Promise

Every repaired board is tested and comes with a service warranty. We offer special wholesale rates and priority support for technicians and mechanics through our B2B program.
